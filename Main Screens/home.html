About IMPN Estates

IMPN Estates is an international property management organization formed through collaboration between professionals from Ireland, Morocco, Palestine, and Nigeria. The name IMPN represents the initials of these countries.

Our system supports property management including clients, properties, sales, bids, and reporting.

Our Core Services

  • Bid Management: Create and manage bids.
  • Sales Processing: Complete property sales.
  • Client Maintenance: Manage client records.
  • Property Management: Manage different property types.
  • Reporting: Generate reports.

Our Vision

IMPN Estates aims to provide an efficient system for property management, focusing on accuracy, transparency, and ease of use.
